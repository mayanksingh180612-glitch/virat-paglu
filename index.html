03 / THE JOURNEY

FROM DELHI TO HISTORY.

The milestones that shaped an extraordinary cricketing legacy.

2008

The Beginning

Captained India to the Under-19 World Cup title and made his ODI debut for India.

2011

World Champion

Won the ICC Cricket World Cup with India and scored a century against Bangladesh in the tournament.

2012

The 183 Against Pakistan

Produced a remarkable ODI innings of 183 in the Asia Cup against Pakistan.

2016

The IPL Season of a Lifetime

Scored 973 runs in the IPL season, setting a record for the most runs in a single edition.

2018

Dominance Around the World

Delivered outstanding performances across formats and led India to a historic Test series win in Australia in 2018–19.

2022

That Unforgettable Melbourne Night

His unbeaten 82 against Pakistan in the T20 World Cup became one of the most celebrated innings in T20 cricket.

2023

Number 50

Scored his 50th ODI century in the World Cup semi-final and finished the tournament with 765 runs.

2024

T20 World Cup Glory

Played a defining role in India's T20 World Cup final victory before retiring from T20 internationals.

2025

Another ICC Trophy

Helped India win the Champions Trophy and announced his retirement from Test cricket.
